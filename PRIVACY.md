# Privacy Policy — Tall Screenshot

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

Tall Screenshot is a browser extension for taking screenshots, picking colors and measuring elements on web pages. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## Data we collect

**None.** Tall Screenshot does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

## What stays on your device

- **Screenshots**: when you take a capture, the images are stored temporarily in the extension's local browser storage (IndexedDB) so the editor can display them. Only the 5 most recent captures are kept, and older ones are deleted automatically. Screenshots leave your device only if you download, copy or share them yourself.
- **Settings and recent colors**: your preferences and the last 16 colors you picked are stored in the browser's local extension storage.

Removing the extension deletes all of this data.

## When the extension accesses web pages

The extension reads a page only when you start a capture or a tool on that tab (by clicking the toolbar button, using a keyboard shortcut or using the right-click menu). It uses the `activeTab` permission, so it has no access to any other tabs or sites.
